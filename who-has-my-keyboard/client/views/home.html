WHO HAS MY KEYBOARD? "You control your character… until someone steals your controls."

OBJECTIVE

FIND THE GOLDEN DUCK 🐤 → PICK IT UP (SPACE) → SURVIVE → REACH THE EXIT 🚦 → WIN 🏆

CONTROLS

W A S D move · SPACE grab the duck · 1 2 3 use a power · TAB scoreboard

POWERS

  • 🖐️ STEAL KEY — a giant hand rips a key off their keyboard
  • 🔄 SWAP KEYS — your keyboard flies over to them and theirs to you
  • 🧠 MIND CONTROL — you drive their character for 5 seconds
  • 🕺 DANCE CURSE — they dance instead of running and drop the duck
  • 🔒 LOCK KEY — a giant lock drops on their W for 9 seconds
  • ⚡ SPEED BOOST — run away with the duck
